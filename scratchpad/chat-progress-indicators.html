What shaped the prototypes

Design rules used here

1

Communicate state, don’t decorate. The indicator replaces the normal chat icon only while generation is active, so motion has a precise meaning.

2

Design at final size. At 14px, silhouette and cadence survive; blur, fine strokes, and complex paths do not. Every option uses a fixed footprint.

3

Prefer compositor-friendly motion. The concepts mainly animate opacity and transform, keeping them smooth while chat streaming taxes the UI.

4

Keep the loop calm. Most cycles sit near 1–1.5 seconds with small amplitude. The title remains the row’s visual anchor.

5

Reduced motion still communicates. Each loader becomes a crisp, static “active” glyph instead of disappearing or strobing.
